# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** June 9, 2026

Trackeroo is an open-source browser extension maintained at [github.com/percdotdev/trackeroo](https://github.com/percdotdev/trackeroo).

## Summary

Trackeroo does not collect, transmit, or sell personal data. Everything stays on your device.

## Data stored locally

The extension uses `browser.storage.local` to remember which CS2 trackers you have enabled or disabled. These preferences are stored only in your browser and are never sent to us or any third party.

## What the extension accesses

Trackeroo runs a content script on `steamcommunity.com` profile pages (`/id/*` and `/profiles/*`). It reads the current page URL to build tracker links. It does not read messages, inventory, friends lists, or other Steam account data.

## Third-party sites

When you click a tracker link, you leave Steam and visit a third-party stats site (CSStats, Leetify, etc.). Those sites have their own privacy policies. Trackeroo does not control them.

## Analytics and tracking

Trackeroo includes no analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.

## Changes

Updates to this policy will be committed to the repository. The "Last updated" date reflects the most recent change.

## Contact

Questions or concerns: [open an issue](https://github.com/percdotdev/trackeroo/issues).
